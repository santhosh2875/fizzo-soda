Nine pictures, no clip

The cans on this site are real 3D, drawn by code, so they need no pictures and no video. The only thing to make is the fruit: one picture for each flavour, with five pieces of fruit on a plain blue background. I cut the pieces out, print them on the cans and let them fly around the page.

The site already works without them. Until your pictures arrive it shows simple drawn fruit. Your pictures replace those with real, photo-quality fruit.
